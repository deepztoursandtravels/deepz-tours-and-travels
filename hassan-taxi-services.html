Hassan Tourist Places – Sightseeing with Photos

Explore some of the best-known attractions in Hassan district. The places below combine heritage, spirituality, scenic landscapes and photography stops. The order of visits should be selected according to your starting point, available time, temple schedules and road conditions.

Chennakeshava Temple, Belur

Belur is celebrated for the Chennakeshava Temple and its detailed Hoysala stone craftsmanship. Visitors can admire carved pillars, sculptural panels and the temple complex. It is one of the signature heritage stops in Hassan district and can be paired with Halebidu on a full-day outing.

Photo source: Hassan District Administration

Hoysaleshwara Temple, Halebidu

Halebidu was an important Hoysala capital. The Hoysaleshwara and Kedareshwara temple complex is known for intricate stone carvings depicting scenes from the Ramayana, Mahabharata and Bhagavata traditions. Combine this stop with Belur after checking temple access and visiting hours.

Photo source: Hassan District Administration

Shravanabelagola – Gommateshwara Bahubali

Shravanabelagola is a major Jain pilgrimage centre, famous for the monumental Gommateshwara Bahubali statue on Vindhyagiri Hill. Visitors climb the hill steps to reach the sacred site, so plan for walking, suitable footwear and sufficient time. Chandragiri Hill is another heritage point in the town.

Photo source: Hassan District Administration

Gorur Dam – Hemavathi Reservoir

Gorur Dam is built across the Hemavathi River and offers a broad reservoir landscape. It is a scenic outing for visitors interested in water views and quiet surroundings. Access to dam areas may be restricted at times; follow local instructions and check access before travelling.

Photo source: Hassan District Administration

Bisle Ghat Viewpoint

Bisle Ghat in Sakleshpur taluk is known for forested slopes and expansive Western Ghats views. It is a nature-focused drive rather than a quick town stop. Weather, visibility, road conditions and local access can affect the experience, especially during heavy rain.

Destination information: Hassan District Administration

Manjarabad Fort, Sakleshpur

The distinctive star-shaped Manjarabad Fort is a well-known historical attraction near Sakleshpur. The fort and surrounding highland scenery make it a popular stop for history lovers and photographers. Check the current entry arrangements and road access before setting out.

Destination information: Hassan District Administration

Shettihalli Rosary Church

This historic Gothic-style church was built in the nineteenth century and is known for its changing appearance beside the Hemavathi reservoir. Water levels vary by season, creating different views of the old structure. Visit carefully, respect safety instructions and avoid entering unsafe water or restricted areas.

Destination information: Hassan District Administration

Hasanamba Temple, Hassan

Hasanamba Temple is an important spiritual landmark in Hassan city. The temple is especially popular during its annual Deepavali-period opening and festival season, when devotees travel from many places. Darshan dates, queue arrangements and entry information should be checked from official temple updates before visiting.

Destination information: Hassan District Administration

Mosale Temples and Hoysala Heritage

For travellers interested in architecture beyond the busiest attractions, the Hassan region has additional Hoysala-era temples and village heritage sites. Mosale and Doddagaddavalli are possible additions depending on your route and time. Confirm access locally and plan these as optional stops.

Photo source: Hassan District Administration (Belur sculpture reference)
