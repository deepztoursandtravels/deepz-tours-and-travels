Chikkamagaluru Tourist Places & Sightseeing Taxi

Explore popular hill stations, waterfalls, temples, lakes and coffee estates with Deepz Tour’s and Travels. Each place is shown with a photo and a short guide. Some landscape photos are representative images; confirm exact site access and photo details before travel. Select your favourite stops and enquire for a sightseeing taxi plan.

Mullayanagiri Peak

Visit Karnataka’s famous high-altitude peak for sweeping Western Ghats views. The final approach may involve steps and local access restrictions.

Seethalayanagiri

A scenic hill viewpoint commonly combined with Mullayanagiri. Enjoy cool weather and mountain landscapes when conditions are clear.

Baba Budangiri / Dattatreya Peeta

Explore the well-known hill destination and spiritual site surrounded by the Western Ghats. Check local timings and road conditions.

Jhari Falls

Enjoy a forest waterfall experience. Access may involve local jeep or shuttle services; confirm current arrangements before travel.

Hirekolale Lake

Relax beside the lake and enjoy views of the surrounding hills. Daylight visits are ideal for scenery and photography.

Kemmanagundi

Plan a nature-focused outing among green hills, gardens and viewpoints. Travel time and route can be planned from your pickup point.

Deviramma Temple

Add this temple stop to your sightseeing itinerary according to opening hours, festival dates and road access.

Kallathigiri Falls

Visit the waterfall and surrounding green landscape when seasonal conditions and access allow. Check the local route before departure.

Amruthapura Temple

Discover the heritage architecture of this historic temple. It can be included in a suitable route with nearby attractions.

Coffee Plantation Visit

Experience Chikkamagaluru’s coffee-growing landscape. Plantation entry and guided visits may require advance permission or booking.

Kelagur Tea Estate

Explore the rolling tea gardens of Kelagur in the Mudigere region. Enjoy plantation scenery and enquire in advance about visitor access or estate experiences.

Samse Tea Estate

Visit the green tea plantation landscape near Samse and Kudremukh. Add this scenic stop to a Kalasa or Horanadu side trip, subject to route and access.

Horanadu – Annapoorneshwari Temple

Visit the renowned Sri Annapoorneshwari Temple amid the Western Ghats. Horanadu is around 10 km from Kalasa; plan temple timings and your route before departure.

Kalasa

Explore the Malnad town known for Kalaseshwara Temple, Bhadra River surroundings and green plantation routes. Kalasa can be combined with Horanadu in a planned trip.

Sringeri – Sharadamba Temple

Visit the famous Sharadamba Temple and Vidyashankara Temple on the banks of the Tunga River. This is a popular spiritual and heritage destination.

Devaramane

Enjoy open grasslands, rolling hill views and a peaceful Western Ghats landscape. The approach may include narrow or rough stretches; confirm vehicle suitability and local access.

Nethravathi Peak Trek

Plan a trek-focused trip to the scenic Nethravathi Peak ridge. Trek permits, local guide rules, weather and access conditions may apply; confirm these before travel.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a one-day Chikkamagaluru sightseeing taxi?

Yes, send your date, pickup point, passenger count and preferred places to enquire about a one-day sightseeing plan.

Can you pick us up from a railway station?

Enquire about pickup from Chikkamagaluru, Kadur, Birur or Tarikere Railway Station. Share train arrival details while requesting the booking.

Which sightseeing places can be covered?

Popular options include Mullayanagiri, Seethalayanagiri, Baba Budangiri, Jhari Falls, Hirekolale Lake, Kemmanagundi, Deviramma Temple, Kallathigiri Falls, Amruthapura Temple, Kelagur Tea Estate, Samse Tea Estate, Horanadu, Kalasa, Sringeri, Devaramane and Nethravathi Peak trek. Confirm route feasibility, permits and access before travel.
