Chikkamagaluru Taxi & Sightseeing

Visit the coffee-growing hills and scenic viewpoints of Chikkamagaluru. Popular itinerary options include:

  • Mullayanagiri Peak
  • Baba Budangiri (Dattatreya Peeta)
  • Seethalayanagiri
  • Jhari Falls
  • Hirekolale Lake
  • Manikyadhara Falls
  • Coffee plantation visits

Hassan Taxi & Heritage Tour

Plan a heritage-focused journey around Hassan district, with stops such as:

  • Belur Chennakeshava Temple
  • Halebeedu Hoysaleswara Temple
  • Shravanabelagola and Bahubali statue
  • Shettihalli Rosary Church
  • Gorur Hemavathi Dam
  • Hasanamba Temple (seasonal access)

Sakleshpur Taxi & Hill Places

Explore Western Ghats scenery and plantation landscapes. Depending on road and local access conditions, consider:

  • Manjarabad Fort
  • Bisle View Point
  • Jenukal Gudda
  • Devaramane
  • Magajahalli Waterfalls
  • Hemavathi backwaters

Kadur & Birur Taxi

Arrange railway station pickup, local transfers and onward trips from Kadur and Birur. Nearby sightseeing options include:

  • Amruthapura Amruteshvara Temple
  • Tarikere region
  • Bhadra reservoir region
  • Chikkamagaluru hill destinations
