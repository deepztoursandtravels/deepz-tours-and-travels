Best Places to Visit Around Kukke Subramanya

Use this list to shortlist stops for your trip. The order and number of attractions should be adjusted to your available time, weather, opening hours and road conditions.

Sri Kukke Subramanya Temple

The main destination for devotees. Check the temple’s official notices for darshan, seva booking, dress guidance and special-day crowd information.

Kumaradhara River

A river associated with the pilgrimage area. Use permitted bathing and access locations and follow safety instructions, especially during heavy rain.

Kumara Parvatha

A well-known trekking destination near Subramanya. This is a demanding trek and needs separate time, fitness preparation and current forest/trek permissions.

Adi Subrahmanya Temple

A nearby shrine often included by pilgrims in their visit. Confirm local timings and route access.

Bisle Ghat viewpoint region

A scenic Western Ghats route that may be considered as an additional stop when road and weather conditions are suitable.

Dharmasthala

A popular temple destination that can be combined with Kukke as a longer multi-stop pilgrimage route. Allow sufficient travel time between locations.
