Best Places to Visit Around Mudigere

Use this list to shortlist stops for your trip. The order and number of attractions should be adjusted to your available time, weather, opening hours and road conditions.

Devaramane

A scenic hill area known for rolling green slopes and panoramic views. Weather can change quickly, so plan with daylight and check local road conditions.

Ettina Bhuja

A striking hill peak popular with trekkers. The hike requires suitable footwear, fitness and current local/forest access guidance; a taxi can be planned to the permitted starting area.

Ghatikallu viewpoint

A scenic viewpoint in the Mudigere region. Check road access and visibility on the day, especially during monsoon conditions.

Rani Jhari viewpoint

A cliffside viewpoint in the wider Mudigere–Kalasa region. Confirm local access, weather and safety before visiting.

Kalasa and Horanadu

Temple destinations that can be included in a longer regional sightseeing route. Check temple timings and allow sufficient travel time.

Coffee estates around Mudigere

The area has coffee-growing landscapes and plantation stays. Entry to private estates requires permission or a confirmed stay/visit arrangement.

Belur and Halebeedu

Heritage temple destinations that can be added to a longer trip from the Mudigere/Chikkamagaluru side, depending on the schedule.
