Shimoga (Shivamogga) Tourist Places to Visit

Shivamogga district is known for the Western Ghats landscape, waterfalls, reservoirs, wildlife experiences and historic temples. Plan a sightseeing taxi from Shimoga city or your hotel and cover the places that fit your available time. Some attractions are outside the city, so confirm the route and visiting conditions before starting.

Jog Falls

See the dramatic Sharavathi River waterfall, known for its four cascades — Raja, Rani, Roarer and Rocket. A popular full-day outing from the Shivamogga region.

Agumbe Sunset Viewpoint

Enjoy hill views, rainforest scenery and sunset panoramas. Add Agumbe to a Thirthahalli-side sightseeing plan, subject to weather and road conditions.

Kodachadri Hills

A well-known Western Ghats trekking and nature destination. The approach and final ascent require advance planning; check local access and jeep or trekking arrangements.

Kundadri Hill

A scenic monolithic hill near the Agumbe–Thirthahalli route, with a Jain temple at the summit and broad views of the surrounding green hills.

Honnemaradu Backwaters

Explore the Sharavathi backwater landscape and nature-based adventure activities. Check activity permissions, operator timings and access rules before visiting.

Tyavarekoppa Tiger and Lion Safari

A wildlife attraction near Shivamogga city. Safari operation, ticketing and visitor timings can change, so confirm with the safari before departure.

Sakrebailu Elephant Camp

Visit the elephant camp on the Shivamogga–Thirthahalli side. Morning visits are commonly preferred; confirm current visitor access and timings before planning.

Mandagadde Bird Sanctuary

A river-island birdwatching spot on the Tunga River route towards Thirthahalli. Seasonal bird activity makes it a pleasant nature stop.

Gajanur Dam

A riverside location near Shivamogga. Public access may be restricted during maintenance or construction, so verify access before adding it to your trip.
